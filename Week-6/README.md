# Enterprise Healthcare Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes healthcare network data using **SQL and Microsoft Power BI** to generate insights into patient demographics, clinical operations, service delivery, and financial performance.

SQL was used to query and analyze healthcare data, while Power BI was used to develop interactive dashboards and communicate key performance indicators (KPIs) to support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze patient demographics and chronic disease patterns.
- Evaluate appointment trends and healthcare service demand.
- Identify common diagnoses, laboratory tests, and prescribed medications.
- Compare healthcare facility and departmental performance.
- Monitor revenue, billing, payments, and insurance claims.
- Identify data-quality issues and recommend operational improvements.

## Tools and Technologies

- **SQL** — data querying, filtering, joins, aggregations, and analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI** — interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** — calculated measures, KPIs, and business metrics.
- **Data Modeling** — relationships between healthcare datasets.

## SQL Analysis

## Key Findings

Based on the supplied dataset summary:

- **20,000** registered patients.
- **14,929** patients recorded with chronic conditions.
- **5,000** recorded appointments.
- **5,487** patients aged above 65 years.
- **70.17 million** in reported revenue during June, the highest monthly figure.
- **57.25 million** in reported revenue during September, the lowest monthly figure.
- **919.95 million** in reported outstanding balances.

Financial figures and clinical counts require validation before being used for formal management decisions.

## Data Quality Considerations

The analysis identified areas requiring further investigation:

- Financial totals across billing, payments, claims, and outstanding balances do not fully reconcile.
- Diagnosis counts may include multiple records for individual patients.
- Repeated phone numbers require validation.
- Insurance coverage records and category completeness should be checked.
- KPI definitions, date ranges, and database relationships should be verified.

## Recommendations

1. Strengthen chronic disease management and patient follow-up.
2. Align staffing and healthcare resources with service demand.
3. Improve insurance claim processing and financial reconciliation.
4. Monitor monthly revenue and facility performance.
5. Establish routine data validation and KPI review procedures.


## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the application of **SQL and Power BI** to healthcare analytics. By combining SQL-based data analysis with interactive dashboards and business intelligence techniques, the project transforms healthcare data into actionable insights that support improved patient care, operational efficiency, and financial accountability.
