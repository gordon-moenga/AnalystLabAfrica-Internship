# SwiftLog Logistics: Business Intelligence and Operational Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses **Power BI** to analyse SwiftLog Logistics' business performance across revenue, customer activity, inventory management, warehouse operations, delivery performance and product returns.

The objective was to transform operational data into interactive dashboards that provide insights into key performance indicators (KPIs), highlight operational challenges and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse revenue performance across products, customers and regions.
- Evaluate monthly revenue and order trends.
- Assess delivery performance and identify shipment delays.
- Examine inventory levels and warehouse activity.
- Analyse product returns and their reported causes.
- Develop strategic recommendations to improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

## Dashboard Features

- **Executive Overview:** Revenue, orders, customers and key performance indicators.
- **Revenue Analysis:** Monthly revenue trends and revenue by location.
- **Customer Analysis:** Customer activity and top customers by revenue.
- **Inventory and Warehouse Analysis:** Inventory value, stock levels and warehouse comparisons.
- **Delivery Performance:** On-time deliveries, delayed shipments and average delivery delay.
- **Returns Analysis:** Returned items categorised by reported reason.

## Key Findings

- Total revenue was approximately **NGN 7.11 million**.
- Approximately **5,000 orders** and **1,000 customers** were reported.
- Lagos contributed **54.26% of total revenue**.
- On-time deliveries represented **63.72%**, while delayed deliveries accounted for **36.28%**.
- Reported inventory value was approximately **NGN 83.80 million**.
- The dashboard recorded **500 returned items**, with late delivery being the most frequently reported return reason.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, data visualisation and KPI reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **DAX:** Measures and business calculations.
- **Data Modelling:** Relationships between relevant datasets.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis highlights opportunities to improve delivery reliability, reduce product returns, strengthen inventory controls, monitor supplier and warehouse performance, and improve customer retention.

Further validation is required for selected dashboard figures, while additional data and measures are needed to reliably evaluate supplier performance, warehouse efficiency and product-specific return rates.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform operational data into meaningful business insights. The resulting dashboards provide a foundation for monitoring performance, identifying operational weaknesses and supporting strategic decisions at SwiftLog Logistics.
